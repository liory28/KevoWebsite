Questions

Before you start.

Still deciding? Ask Kevo anything inside the app, any time.

What is a DSCR loan?

An investment-property loan that qualifies on the property's rent compared with its monthly payment (the debt service coverage ratio), instead of your personal income or tax returns.

Will checking affect my credit score?

No. Kevo uses a soft credit check, which doesn't affect your score.

What do I need to get started?

The property address, price and expected rent. Then you'll confirm your credit, connect a bank account (read-only) and tell Kevo about any investment properties you own now or have owned.

What kinds of properties qualify?

Investment properties only: single-family homes and townhomes, condos, and 2–4 unit buildings. Kevo doesn't finance primary residences or second homes.

Can I close in my LLC?

Yes. You can close in your own name or in your LLC.

Pre-approved or Fully Underwritten: what's the difference?

A pre-approval verifies your credit, funds and experience against the lender's guidelines. Fully Underwritten adds underwriting sign-off before you offer, which is strong enough that many buyers choose to waive the loan contingency. Both remain subject to appraisal and title.

Do I have to talk to anyone?

No. You can go from address to letter on your own. A real person is available whenever you want one.
